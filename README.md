# Dimension Expansion

[![Modrinth](https://badges.moddingx.org/modrinth/downloads/lWgCtgxh)](https://modrinth.com/mod/dimension-expansion)
[![CurseForge](https://badges.moddingx.org/curseforge/downloads/1306668)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dimension-expansion)

**Dimension Expansion** adds multiple fully data-driven dimensions to Minecraft 1.21.1 — inspired by the Deep Dark, The Beneath, and classic Mining Dimensions.

---

## Features

### **The Deep Beneath**

A single-biome dimension cloaked in darkness.

* Normal torches **burn out over time** — bring alternative light sources.
* The **darkness itself deals damage** if you linger unlit.
* Hostile mobs spawn more often and have **extra health**.
* Ores are **plentiful**, making this an excellent (if dangerous) mining realm.

### **The Mining Dimension**

A peaceful alternative for resource gathering.

* A **superflat-style dimension** with layers of Stone, Deepslate, Netherrack, and End Stone.
* **Completely free of hostile mobs** for relaxed automation or mining.
* Higher Y-level terrain (Y=208) for large-scale building and automation.

### **The Stone Block Dimension** *(concept stage)*

A builder’s dream or miner’s challenge.

* **Solid from bedrock to bedrock** (-64 to 320).
* Filled entirely with **stone and deepslate**, loaded with ores.

---

## Data-Driven & Customizable

Every aspect of Dimension Expansion’s worlds can be customized through datapacks:

* Ores and biome features
* Mob behavior and spawn rates
* Noise settings, block palettes, and light rules

For modpack makers, this means you can reshape each world without ever touching code.

See the [GitHub Wiki](https://github.com/darklotus781/Dimension-Expansion/wiki) for guides on datapack customization, noise settings, and mod integration.

---

## Installation

1. Install **NeoForge** for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Drop the mod JAR into your `mods/` folder.
3. Launch the game once to generate config and data folders.

---

* **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
